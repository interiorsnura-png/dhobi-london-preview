Client Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

How does concierge and unattended collection work?

expand_more
If you live in a portered mansion building (such as Dolphin Square or Chelsea Cloisters), you can simply leave your items with the front desk concierge labeled "Dhobi London". Our zero-emission van drivers are familiar with all major SW1/SW3 desk protocols. You receive an automated SMS receipt with photographs of your garments as soon as we collect.

What is your standard turnaround time?

expand_more
Standard dry cleaning and Jermyn Street shirt laundry is returned within 48 hours. Fine bed linens and goose down duvets take 72 hours to ensure thorough multi-stage thermal chamber drying and loft fluffing. Urgent 24-hour express service is available on request.

Can I visit your atelier in person on Warwick Way?

expand_more
Yes! We have welcomed clients at 57B Warwick Way, Pimlico since 1994. You are very welcome to drop off garments directly, discuss complex tailoring alterations with our resident master tailor, or bring delicate couture for on-the-spot inspection.

What solvents do you use for dry cleaning?

expand_more
We operate a 100% perchloroethylene-free (Perc-free) atelier. We utilise gentle eco-friendly hydrocarbon and advanced wet-cleaning technologies. This eliminates chemical odours, prolongs natural fabric fibres (fine wools, silks, and cashmere), and prevents colour fading.

Do you offer 24-hour emergency turnaround?

expand_more
Yes. If you have an unexpected business meeting, dinner, or travel commitment, please contact our concierge line at 020 7720 3118 or select "Express 24h" during online booking. A small express surcharge applies.
