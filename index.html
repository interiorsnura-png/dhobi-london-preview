08 / Good to know

A few questions, beautifully answered.

Everything you need to feel comfortable taking the first step.

How do I book a collection?

Use the official booking button to open Dhobi London's current account and booking portal. Select your postcode, service and an available collection window there. The V5 preview itself does not place orders.

Can my concierge receive or hand over garments?

You can request a concierge handover or an authorised safe place. Please confirm the arrangement and any building restrictions during actual booking.

How quickly will my garments be returned?

Many everyday garments are advertised with a 48-hour aim, but specialist fabrics, alterations and bedding can take longer. Confirm turnaround when you book.

Are the prices on this page final?

No. The figures are indicative and have not been approved as final tariffs. The Dhobi team or official booking portal will confirm current rates, VAT, minimum order and delivery terms.

Do you cover my London postcode?

The site highlights areas in Central and South West London, but route and time-slot eligibility depend on your full address. The official booking service provides the final answer.
